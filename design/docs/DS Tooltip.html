Components / Tooltip

Tooltip

A short label that appears on hover or keyboard focus, naming or clarifying its trigger. Tooltips carry supplementary text only — nothing a user must read to proceed, and never anything interactive.

Betahover · focus · 4 placements

Anatomy

A dark neutral bubble (--n-900) with white 12px text, 6×10px padding, small radius, and an optional 8px arrow pointing at the trigger. One or two short lines — a name, a shortcut, or a full value.

Placement

Top is the default. Flip to bottom when there's no room above; left/right only for triggers at a viewport edge. The arrow always points at the trigger's center.

Tooltip vs. popover: if it needs a heading, an action, a link, or should stay open — it's not a tooltip. Use a menu or modal instead. Tooltips are text-only and self-dismissing.

Do & don't

Do

Label every icon-only button with a tooltip. Show full values for truncated text. Keep it to a few words.

Don't

Don't put required information, links, or buttons in a tooltip — and never attach one to a disabled control's dead zone; wrap the trigger instead.

Accessibility

  • The bubble uses role="tooltip" and is referenced from the trigger via aria-describedby.
  • Triggers must be focusable — tooltips appear on keyboard focus exactly as on hover, with no delay.
  • Escape dismisses without moving focus; the tooltip never traps or receives focus itself.
  • Content is plain text available to screen readers via the description — never the only place information lives.
  • White-on---n-900 passes contrast at 12px; motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.

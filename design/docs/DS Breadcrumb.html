Components / Breadcrumb

Breadcrumb

The trail at the top of every app page showing where you are and how to step back up. It mirrors the sidebar's hierarchy in text, and its last item always names the current page.

Betaevery page

Anatomy

Links separated by a faint slash, ending in the current page as non-link bold-ish text. Parent items are muted and clickable; an org context shows its logo glyph.

Workspace/My Brags
Account/Security
Organisations/A Acme Corp/Members
parent link · slash separator · current page (not a link).

Behaviour

  • Last item is the page — current, not a link, matching the page's h1.
  • Parents are links that step up the hierarchy; they mirror the sidebar grouping.
  • Org pages carry the org as a middle crumb with its logo glyph, linking to its Overview.
  • Single level deep — most pages are two crumbs; org sections are three. Never more.
Breadcrumb mirrors the sidebar. The first crumb is the sidebar group (Workspace, Account, an org); the breadcrumb is the textual echo of where the active nav item lives.

Do & don't

Do
Organisations/A Acme/Settings

Two to three crumbs, ending in the current page.

Don't
Home/App/Orgs/Acme/Settings

Don't stack four-plus levels or make the current page a link.

Accessibility

  • Wrapped in <nav aria-label="Breadcrumb"> with an ordered list of items.
  • The current page uses aria-current="page" and is not a link.
  • Separators are decorative (aria-hidden), never read aloud.
  • Truncates with an ellipsis on narrow screens while keeping the first and last crumb.

Card

A card groups one coherent set of content or one settings concern into a bordered surface. Cards are the backbone of every screen in syoksheet — the brag, the settings section, the org summary are all cards.

Beta 3 types

Anatomy

A structured card has three regions. Header and footer are fixed; the body flexes. A content card (like a brag) keeps the same border + radius but composes its own internals.

Hover lifts the card 2px with a medium shadow. The whole card is the click target.

Behavior

  • Interactive cards lift on hover — translateY(-2px) + --shadow-md, 160ms. Static cards never move.
  • Whole-card links: the entire surface is clickable; nested buttons/links stop propagation.
  • Secondary detail expands inline — dates, verifiers and the anchor reveal in place, not in a drawer.
  • One concern per card. If a card needs two Save buttons, it's two cards.

No header, and the action is left-aligned — inconsistent with every other card.

Every titled card has a header and a footer. If there's nothing to save, the card is a content card and may omit the footer — but a settings card always shows both.

Accessibility

  • Card titles are real headings (<h2>/<h3>) so the page outline is navigable.
  • Whole-card links wrap a single <a> or use a clickable region with a focusable primary link inside — never a click handler with no keyboard path.
  • Hover lift is decorative; never hide information that only appears on hover from keyboard or touch users — provide an inline expand control too.
  • Maintain ≥4.5:1 text contrast on the white surface and the tinted footer alike.

Modal & dialog

A modal interrupts the flow to demand a focused decision or a short, self-contained task. It's the heaviest interruption in the system, so it's reserved for moments that genuinely can't happen inline.

Beta 2 types

Anatomy

A dimmed scrim over the page, a centred surface with a header (optional status icon + title), a body, and a right-aligned footer of actions. The primary/confirming action is right-most, exactly like a card footer.

Unlock this brag?

Unlocking returns it to draft and removes all 3 verifications. You'll need to request verification again.
scrim · surface · header (icon + title) · body · footer (Cancel + confirm).

Types

Two only. A confirmation dialog for a single yes/no decision, and a task modal for a short form. Anything longer belongs on its own page or an inline section.

Prefer inline over modal. Routine settings expand in place as sections — reach for a modal only when the decision must block everything else (e.g. unlock, disband, leave).

Destructive confirmation

High-consequence actions add a type-to-confirm gate: the danger button stays disabled until the user types an exact phrase. This prevents reflexive clicks on irreversible actions.

Disband Acme Corp?

Releases DNS verification, removes the public wall, and unbinds all 84 brags. This cannot be undone.
Danger button disabled until the typed phrase matches exactly.

Behaviour

  • Focus moves in on open (to the first field, or Cancel for a confirmation) and is trapped inside until close.
  • Dismiss via Cancel, the scrim, or Esc — all three close a modal. A destructive type-to-confirm still requires the typed phrase to act, but can always be cancelled.
  • Scrim is rgba(10,10,11,.45) with a 2px blur; the page beneath does not scroll.
  • Enter/exit at 160–200ms — surface scales from 0.96 + fades, scrim fades. No bounce.
  • One modal at a time — never stack. A modal never opens another modal.

Do & don't

Do

Keep it to one decision or one short form. Title asks the question; confirm button echoes it ("Leave Acme Corp").

Don't

Don't put multi-step settings, long forms, or tabbed content in a modal — use a page or inline sections instead.

Content

  • Title is the question — "Unlock this brag?", "Disband Acme Corp?"
  • Body states the consequence concretely, bolding what's lost ("removes all 3 verifications").
  • Confirm button echoes the title's verb + object, never "OK" or "Yes".
  • Cancel is always present and always ghost.

Accessibility

  • Container is role="dialog" with aria-modal="true" and an aria-labelledby pointing at the title.
  • Focus is trapped while open and returns to the trigger on close.
  • Esc closes; the scrim is click-to-close but never the only path.
  • Background content gets aria-hidden / inert so it's skipped by AT and tab order.

Components / Menu

Menu

A popover list of actions or navigation triggered by a button — most often the ⋯ overflow on a card or row, or an account menu. It holds actions, not form values (use a Select for choosing a value).

Betaoverflow · account

Anatomy

A trigger (icon button or labeled button) opens a floating panel of items. Items have an optional icon + label; destructive items are clay and sit last, below a separator.

Menu holds actions; Select holds values. If picking an item changes a form field, that's a Select. If it performs something (edit, leave, sign out), it's a Menu.

Behavior

  • Opens from its trigger, anchored to it; flips above/left near a viewport edge.
  • One open at a time; opening another closes the first.
  • Closes on item click, outside click, or Esc.
  • Role-gated items appear only when permitted (Owner sees "Transfer ownership", Member sees "Leave").
  • No nesting beyond one submenu level; prefer flat menus.

Do & don't

Do

Group related actions; put the destructive one last, separated and in clay.

Don't

Don't hide the only primary action in a menu, or fill it with form inputs and toggles.

Accessibility

  • Trigger is a <button> with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded; icon-only triggers need an aria-label.
  • Panel is role="menu" with role="menuitem" children; full arrow-key navigation and type-ahead.
  • Focus moves into the menu on open and returns to the trigger on close.
  • Destructive items are distinguished by label and position, not color alone.

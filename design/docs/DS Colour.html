Colour

syoksheet is a white-paper product with one decisive brand colour. Colour is used sparingly and always with meaning — never for decoration. Forest green is reserved exclusively for verification.

Beta 4 roles

Neutral · the paper

A pure white-to-near-black ramp carries surfaces, text and hairlines. No tint. Structure comes from these neutrals and 1px borders, not from fills or shadows.

Usage rules

  • Colour from the mark, not the panel. Tint an icon or badge; keep the surface flat. Never a gradient panel with matching coloured text.
  • Teal ≠ forest. Teal is action/brand; forest is verified. They never substitute for each other, even though both are green-ish.
  • Status colours are semantic. Amber and clay only ever signal pending and destructive — never used to add visual interest.
  • Most of any screen is neutral. If colour covers more than a small fraction of a view, something is wrong.

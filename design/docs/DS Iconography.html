Iconography & motion

Line icons and quiet motion. One stroke weight, one icon family, currentColor throughout; movement is fast, purposeful, and uses a single easing curve. Nothing bounces or decorates.

Beta line · 1.75

Icon style

A single outline family (Feather-style): 24px viewBox, 1.75 stroke, round caps and joins, no fill. Icons inherit currentColor so they always match their context.

Motion

Motion is quick and quiet. Three durations cover everything, with one easing curve for movement and plain ease for fades. Hover a chip to feel the card lift.

Do & don't

Do

One outline family, 1.75 stroke, currentColor. Consistent everywhere.

Don't
🎉

No filled glyphs, mismatched weights, or emoji standing in for icons.

Accessibility & reduced motion

  • Decorative icons are aria-hidden; an icon-only control needs an aria-label.
  • Icons never carry meaning alone — they accompany a label or a clear context.
  • Honour prefers-reduced-motion: transforms and slides drop to simple fades or none.
  • No infinite or looping decorative animation; motion always resolves to a still state.

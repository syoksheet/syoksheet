rules
· The sidebar is not collapsible. It holds a fixed width per breakpoint: 260px ≥1440, 230px 1120–1439, 200px 840–1119, 180px below 840.
· It is always flex-shrink:0. It never squeezes between breakpoints — the content column absorbs all remaining space.
· Rows never wrap. Labels get min-width:0 and ellipsize; icons, counts, chevrons and org tiles never shrink.
· Group headings, counts and the org accordion stay visible at every width.
